Is Convenience
Priced In?

Food access and HDB resale prices across Singapore

Does living near a supermarket or hawker centre coincide with higher HDB resale prices? I compare January-June 2021 transactions with the course datasets on food outlets and 500-metre service areas. The maps show prices, flat sizes and addresses outside the supplied coverage. A photograph of Yuhua Village Market and Food Centre adds a closer look at one place. The price comparison is descriptive; it does not measure the effect of food access alone.

By Wang Yueyao

Explore the map ↓

About the data. HDB points represent addresses with resale transactions recorded from January to June 2021, not households or population. The 500-metre layer is the service-area dataset supplied for this assignment. The facility and service-area dates are not specified. Access status comes from the supplied outside-area subset; it is not a new routing calculation. Results are descriptive and do not establish causation.
