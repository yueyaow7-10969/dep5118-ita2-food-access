Is Convenience
Priced In?

Food access and HDB resale prices across Singapore

How much is everyday food access reflected in Singapore's public housing market? This story combines January-June 2021 HDB resale records with supermarkets, hawker centres and 500-metre service areas. It maps where food options are close, how prices and flat sizes vary, and which addresses fall beyond the threshold. The citywide price gap is modest, but access varies sharply between towns - showing that food convenience is a neighbourhood planning issue, not a simple proxy for expensive housing.

By Wang Yueyao

About the data. HDB points represent addresses with resale transactions recorded from January to June 2021, not households or population. The 500-metre layer is the service-area dataset supplied for this assignment. Results are descriptive and do not establish causation.
